A companion for your live stream

AudienceTTS reads live YouTube chat aloud on your Mac. It follows your OBS stream, so your attention can stay with the people watching.

macOS · YouTube API Services · Local speech

One connected workflow

From stream start
to spoken chat.

  1. 01

    OBS sets the rhythm

    Start your stream in OBS. After one-time setup, AudienceTTS follows the stream through a connection on the same Mac.

  2. 02

    YouTube delivers the chat

    Connect your channel through Google’s sign-in screen. The app finds its active broadcast and receives live chat through the official YouTube API.

  3. 03

    Your Mac speaks

    Your filters, installed macOS voice and selected audio output shape what you hear. OBS stream stop cancels speech and clears pending messages.

Your stream, your controls

Keep your familiar
monitoring setup.

Safari can remain open for YouTube Studio, chat and stream health. The direct YouTube connection does not need a second browser session to read messages.

Pause speech at any time. Stop TTS for the current stream, reconnect when needed, and choose a broadcast if more than one is active. These controls do not start or stop your OBS stream.

Understand the connection

Read-only access.
Clear data practices.

AudienceTTS requests read-only YouTube access. Account tokens and your OBS connection password are stored in macOS Keychain after you allow secure connections.

Using AudienceTTS

AudienceTTS is an independent desktop application that uses YouTube API Services. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, YouTube or OBS.

By using its YouTube-connected features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This informational website does not sign you in, connect to your channel or collect chat. For help or privacy questions, use AudienceTTS support issues. GitHub issues are public: do not post credentials, OAuth client files, private chat content or unreviewed logs.
